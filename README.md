One word for your Wi-Fi. This entry picks the word the Wi-Fi itself has
been saying all along — the one that appears at the worst second of every
film, call and download, and needs no further explanation from anyone who
has ever lived with a router.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the word to `entry.txt` in the
output directory, clearing the directory's contents first and adding no
trailing newline.
